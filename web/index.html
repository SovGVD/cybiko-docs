Cybiko converters

Open, convert and make Cybiko Xtreme files. Everything runs in this page: no file leaves your computer. The same converters exist as Python tools and give the same bytes; the formats are in file-formats.md.

Packs and archives

EZ Loader packs (.cap, .csu), CyOS archives (.app, .dl, MXD clips) and flash dumps. Drop one in to see and save what it holds; a part in a format this page knows has an Open button.

Make an archive

Pack files into a CyOS archive (an application is a main.e, a root.inf, icons and resources). Each part is compressed with CyOS's method 2 unless you tick store or it doesn't get smaller; root.inf goes first.

Pictures

.pic pictures (four greys, the Cybiko's own) and .xpic (13 greys, for Photo Viewer).

Picture to Cybiko

Any picture the browser opens. PNG, BMP, PGM and PPM are read exactly as the Python tool reads them, so the result is the same file; JPEG, GIF, WebP and others go through the browser's decoder.

Melodies

.mus and .music melodies (the same format) to and from text and MIDI, played the way the Cybiko's speaker plays them.

Choose or drop a .mus, .music, .mid or a melody as text
CyOS's sound envelopes (for version 2 melodies)

Version 2 melodies use one of the 60 envelopes in CyOS's melody player (1.amp to 60.amp). They are Cybiko's, so this page doesn't have them: choose a dump of the Cybiko's flash (the page finds the melody player's archive in it) or the .amp files. Without them, version 2 melodies play as plain tones.

Choose a flash dump or .amp files

No envelopes loaded.

Text

The melody as text you can edit. One item a line: a note and its length in ticks of 10 ms (C#5 10; C4 is middle C), rest 5, glide D5 and vibrato 1 after a note, loop 0 3 … next 0 (a part played 3 + 1 times), raw 0xf8 0. Version 2 also has pulse, volume and envelope lines.

Sound envelopes

.amp envelopes shape the loudness of the notes of version 2 melodies. This reads them and writes Cybiko Converter's text (.a), and makes an .amp from that text. CyOS only uses its own 60, so a new one is only heard by replacing one of those.

Choose or drop an .amp or an .a text

Recordings

CyRecord and CyTalk record IMA ADPCM .wav files at 8000 Hz. A recording becomes an ordinary 16-bit WAV; any 8 or 16-bit PCM WAV (any rate, mono or stereo) becomes a recording. Other sound files (MP3, OGG...) are decoded by the browser first.

Choose or drop a sound file

MXD clips

Video clips for the MP3 cartridge: a 160 x 100 picture in four greys, up to five frames a second, over an MP3 sound track. They are CyOS applications; put one on the cartridge's card.

Play a clip

Choose or drop an MXD clip (.app)

Make a clip

The clip's player program (main.e) is the free one made for these tools; or take Cybiko's own from one of the clips on the MP3 cartridge CD (flavor.app, free.app...). Both need the cartridge's software on the Cybiko. The picture comes from the browser's video decoder, so it can differ slightly from the Python tool's (which uses ffmpeg); the rest is the same.
